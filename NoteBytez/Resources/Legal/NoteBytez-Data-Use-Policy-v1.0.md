# NoteBytez Data Use Policy (Privacy Policy)

**Version 1.0 · Last Updated: September 19, 2026**

This Data Use Policy ("Policy") explains how KwickSync ("KwickSync," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the NoteBytez application (the "App"). This Policy is the Privacy Policy incorporated by reference into the NoteBytez End User License Agreement (the "EULA"). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the EULA.

This Policy applies to End Users worldwide, including those in the European Union, United Kingdom, and United States.

## 1. Our Data Philosophy

NoteBytez is built to be fully serverless. KwickSync operates no backend servers of its own. All storage, sync, sharing, push notifications, and subscription entitlement checks happen directly between your device and Apple's own infrastructure - Apple CloudKit, the App Store, and Apple Push Notification service (APNs). Because of this architecture, KwickSync does not access, and has no ability to access, the notes or other Content you create in the App.

## 2. Content You Create

Your notes and other Content are stored in your personal Apple iCloud account, using CloudKit as the storage and sync mechanism, and on your own devices. KwickSync never receives a copy of your Content and cannot view, access, or retrieve it.

When you use the App's sharing feature to share a note with another NoteBytez user, that Content is transmitted directly between your device, Apple's CloudKit infrastructure, and the recipient's device. This is Content sharing that you initiate and control - it is not KwickSync sharing your Content with a third party, and KwickSync does not have visibility into what you share or with whom.

Because KwickSync does not have access to your Content, KwickSync never sells, licenses, or shares your Content with any third party.

## 3. Other Information KwickSync Does Collect

While KwickSync has no access to your Content, there is a limited set of other information KwickSync does have visibility into:

- **Subscription and purchase status.** Apple reports your subscription entitlement status (e.g., active, expired, cancelled) to the App so it can grant or deny access to NoteBytez's features. KwickSync does not receive your payment card or other billing details - those are handled entirely by Apple.
- **Customer support communications.** If you contact KwickSync for support (for example, by email), KwickSync will see the content of that communication, including your name, email address, and any other information you choose to include.
- **Crash and diagnostic analytics.** If you opt in to sharing analytics with app developers through your device's iOS/macOS settings, KwickSync may receive aggregated, typically anonymized crash reports and performance diagnostics from Apple. This is controlled entirely by your device settings, not by the App.

KwickSync does not collect or have access to any other personal information. KwickSync does not use your information for advertising or marketing, does not send marketing or promotional communications, and does not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

## 4. How KwickSync Uses This Information

KwickSync uses the limited information described in Section 3 only to:

- Verify your subscription and grant or restrict access to the App accordingly;
- Respond to and resolve support requests you submit; and
- Identify and fix crashes or performance issues, where diagnostic data is shared with us through your own device settings.

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR/UK GDPR)

For End Users in the European Union and United Kingdom, KwickSync processes information under the following lawful bases:

- **Performance of a contract** - to verify your subscription and provide the App's functionality.
- **Legitimate interests** - to respond to support requests and to diagnose and fix crashes or performance issues, where these interests are not overridden by your own rights and interests.

## 6. Data Controller, Processors, and International Transfers

KwickSync is the data controller for the limited information described in Section 3. Apple acts as KwickSync's infrastructure and data processor for CloudKit, the App Store, and APNs. Because NoteBytez is distributed worldwide, Apple's infrastructure may store and process information in countries other than your own. Apple maintains its own safeguards for these international transfers, including Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable. KwickSync does not itself transfer your Content anywhere, because KwickSync never receives it.

As KwickSync's processing is occasional, does not involve special categories of data at scale, and is not large-scale, KwickSync relies on the exemption in Article 27(2) of the GDPR and does not maintain a designated representative in the EU or UK.

## 7. Data Retention

- **Your Content** remains in your iCloud account and on your devices indefinitely, including after your subscription or account is terminated. The App always gives you read-only access to and the ability to export your existing Content, with no forced deletion timeline, consistent with Section 15 of the EULA. KwickSync has no role in this at all, as it never holds a copy of your Content to delete in the first place.
- **Support communications** are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your inquiry and for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping purposes.
- **Subscription status** is retained only as long as needed to determine your current access to the App.

## 8. Children's Privacy

NoteBytez is not directed at children under the age of 13, and KwickSync does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a resident of the European Union or United Kingdom and are under the age of digital consent in your country (which ranges from 13 to 16 depending on the country), you may only use the App with the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian.

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

**If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom**, you have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a portable copy of any personal information KwickSync holds about you, and the right to object to or restrict certain processing. Because KwickSync has no access to your Content, these rights apply only to the limited information described in Section 3 (subscription status, support communications, and diagnostics).

**If you are a California resident**, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion of that information, and to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. KwickSync does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act. You may also report complaints under California Civil Code Section 1789.3 as described in Section 19 of the EULA.

To exercise any of these rights, contact KwickSync using the information in Section 11.

## 10. Security

Apple CloudKit encrypts your Content both in transit and at rest. Because KwickSync never receives a copy of your Content, it never resides on any server KwickSync controls. The limited information described in Section 3 is protected using secure protocols in transit and, where stored, is encrypted at rest.

## 11. Contact Us

Questions about this Policy, or requests to exercise your privacy rights, may be directed to KwickSync through the contact information provided in the App Store listing for NoteBytez.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

KwickSync may update this Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by other reasonable means before the changes take effect, consistent with Section 21 of the EULA. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy.
