## Purpose

Understand why NoteBytez blocked access and how to fix it.

## Prerequisites

NoteBytez shows a full-screen block.

## Steps

1. **Subscribe to continue**: you have not subscribed. Start your free trial.
2. **Your subscription has ended**: the grace period is over. Resubscribe; nothing has been deleted.
3. **Please reconnect to continue**: NoteBytez could not verify your subscription for a while. Connect to the internet once.
4. **This copy can't be verified**: NoteBytez could not confirm it was installed from the App Store. Reinstall it from the App Store.

{% admonition "tip" %}On every blocked screen your notes are safe and you can choose **Export My Notes**.{% endadmonition %}

## Expected result

You know what to do to unlock NoteBytez.
