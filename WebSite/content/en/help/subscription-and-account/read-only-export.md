## Purpose

Export every note even when your subscription has ended.

## Prerequisites

NoteBytez shows a blocked screen.

## Steps

1. On the blocked screen, choose **Export My Notes**.
2. Choose a folder.

## Expected result

All your notes are saved as Markdown. Nothing has been deleted.
