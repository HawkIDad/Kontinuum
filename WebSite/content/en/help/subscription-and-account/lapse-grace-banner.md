## Purpose

Understand the banner shown after a subscription lapses.

## Prerequisites

Your subscription has lapsed.

## Steps

1. Look at the strip above the app: **Your NoteBytez subscription has lapsed**, with the days of access left.
2. Choose **Resubscribe** to keep full access.
3. If you do nothing, access is blocked when the grace period ends.

## Expected result

You know how long you have, and resubscribing restores full access.
