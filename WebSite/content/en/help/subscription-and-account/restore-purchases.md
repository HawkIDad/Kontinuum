## Purpose

Restore your subscription on a new or reinstalled device.

## Prerequisites

You have subscribed before with the same Apple Account.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**, **Subscription**, and choose **Restore Purchases**. You can also choose it on the subscribe screen.
2. Wait for confirmation.

## Expected result

Your subscription is recognised and NoteBytez unlocks.
