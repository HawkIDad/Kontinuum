## Purpose

Subscribe to NoteBytez and start your free trial.

## Prerequisites

An Apple Account.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Subscription**, then **Change or Start Subscription**. On a fresh install the subscribe screen appears by itself.
2. Choose the monthly or annual plan.
3. Confirm with Apple. Your free trial starts.

## Expected result

NoteBytez is unlocked on all devices signed in to your Apple Account, and through Family Sharing.
