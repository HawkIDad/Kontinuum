## Purpose

Open the Task Dashboard to see the tasks across every note.

## Prerequisites

Some notes contain tasks.

## Steps

1. On iPhone, choose **Explore**, then **Tasks**. On iPad and Mac, choose **Tasks** under Explore. From the journal you can also choose **Tasks** in the toolbar.
2. Read the list. Each row shows the task, its note and, when set, its due date and priority.
3. Tap a checkbox to mark a task done, or tap a task's note to open it.

## Expected result

You see all tasks from all notes, and can complete them from here.
