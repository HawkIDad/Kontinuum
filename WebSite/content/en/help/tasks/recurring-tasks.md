## Purpose

Understand what happens when you complete a recurring task.

## Prerequisites

A task with a repeat rule (daily, weekly, monthly or every N days).

## Steps

1. Find the recurring task on the Task Dashboard; a repeat badge shows its rule.
2. Mark it done.
3. Look at its due date: it advances to the next occurrence.

{% admonition "note" %}This version of NoteBytez shows and advances repeats, but does not yet have a control for setting one.{% endadmonition %}

## Expected result

The task stays completed for this occurrence and its due date moves to the next one.
