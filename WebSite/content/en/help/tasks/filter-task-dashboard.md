## Purpose

Narrow the Task Dashboard and save a filter for reuse.

## Prerequisites

The Task Dashboard is open.

## Steps

1. Choose a status: **Open**, **Done** or **All**.
2. Choose a date: **Any**, **Overdue**, **Due Today** or **Due This Week**. You can also filter by tag.
3. To keep this filter, choose **Save These Filters**, name it and choose **Save**.

## Expected result

The list matches your filters, and a saved filter appears under Saved Views.
