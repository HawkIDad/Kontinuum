## Purpose

Understand how due dates and priority appear on the Task Dashboard.

## Prerequisites

The Task Dashboard has tasks.

## Steps

1. Open the Task Dashboard.
2. Look at each row: a calendar label shows a due date, and a flag labelled P plus a number shows priority.
3. Use the **Overdue**, **Due Today** and **Due This Week** filters to narrow the list by due date.

{% admonition "note" %}This version of NoteBytez displays and filters by due date and priority, but does not yet have a control for setting them.{% endadmonition %}

## Expected result

You can see and filter tasks by due date.
