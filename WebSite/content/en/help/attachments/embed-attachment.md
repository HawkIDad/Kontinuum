## Purpose

Attach an image or PDF to a note.

## Prerequisites

A note is open, and you are signed in to iCloud.

## Steps

1. Choose the **Add Attachment** button (a paperclip) at the bottom of the note.
2. Choose an image or a PDF.
3. The attachment appears in the note.

## Expected result

The file is attached and shows as a thumbnail with its file name.
