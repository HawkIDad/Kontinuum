## Purpose

Open an attached image or PDF full screen.

## Prerequisites

A note has an attachment.

## Steps

1. Find the attachment in the note.
2. Tap its thumbnail.
3. Close the preview when you are done.

## Expected result

The image or PDF opens in a preview.
