## Purpose

Understand where your attached files live.

## Prerequisites

None.

## Steps

1. Add an attachment to a note.
2. NoteBytez stores the file in its iCloud container, not inside the note's text.
3. If iCloud is unavailable (signed out, disabled), NoteBytez cannot attach files.

## Expected result

Attachments sync with iCloud alongside your notes.
