## Purpose

Understand what happens to the files that could not be migrated.

## Prerequisites

A migration summary that lists items under **Not Migrated**.

## Steps

1. In the Migration Assistant, look at **Not Migrated — View Original Archive**.
2. Items that NoteBytez does not convert are kept in an archive of your originals.
3. Your original vault is not changed.

## Expected result

Nothing is lost: what could not be converted is still available.
