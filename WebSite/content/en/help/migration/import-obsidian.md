## Purpose

Bring an Obsidian vault into a new NoteBytez library.

## Prerequisites

An Obsidian vault folder on your device or in iCloud Drive.

## Steps

1. On the library screen, choose **Migration Assistant (Obsidian/Logseq)**.
2. Choose your vault folder. NoteBytez detects that it is an Obsidian vault.
3. Review the summary: **Notes**, **Canvases**, and anything under **Not Migrated**.
4. Choose **Confirm Migration**.

## Expected result

A new library contains your notes, links, tasks and canvases.
