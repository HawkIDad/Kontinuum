## Purpose

Confirm that your notes, links and tasks came across.

## Prerequisites

You have completed a migration.

## Steps

1. Open **Notebooks** and **All Notes** and compare with your old vault.
2. Open a note and check its links in **Backlinks**.
3. Open **Tasks** and **Canvas** to check tasks and boards.

## Expected result

You have confirmed what was imported.
