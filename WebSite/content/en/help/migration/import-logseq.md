## Purpose

Bring a Logseq graph into a new NoteBytez library.

## Prerequisites

A Logseq graph folder on your device or in iCloud Drive.

## Steps

1. On the library screen, choose **Migration Assistant (Obsidian/Logseq)**.
2. Choose your Logseq folder.
3. Review the summary and choose **Confirm Migration**.

## Expected result

A new library contains your Logseq pages.
