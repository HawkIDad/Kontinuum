## Purpose

Resolve a note that was edited on two devices.

## Prerequisites

The sync icon shows a conflict.

## Steps

1. Open **Sync Status** and choose the conflict under **Needs Your Attention**.
2. With **Keep All Versions**, compare the versions and choose **Keep This Version**, or choose **Keep Both Versions**.
3. With **Markdown Diff-Merge**, review the differences and choose **Save Merged Note**.
4. With **Last-Write-Wins + Banner**, open the note; the banner says which edit was kept. Choose **Revert** to undo it, or dismiss the banner.

## Expected result

The conflict is cleared and Sync Status shows **No conflicts — you're all synced**.
