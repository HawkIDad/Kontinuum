## Purpose

Start a sync when you do not want to wait.

## Prerequisites

You are online and signed in to iCloud.

## Steps

1. Choose the sync icon to open **Sync Status**.
2. Choose **Sync Now**.
3. Watch **Recent Activity** for the result.

{% admonition "tip" %}On iPad and Mac you can also run **Sync Now** from the Command Palette.{% endadmonition %}

## Expected result

NoteBytez syncs immediately and records the result.
