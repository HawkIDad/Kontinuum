## Purpose

See what sync has done and whether anything failed.

## Prerequisites

None.

## Steps

1. Open **Sync Status**.
2. Scroll to **Recent Activity**. Each row has a time and what happened.
3. If an error banner appears, choose **Retry**, or dismiss it.

## Expected result

You can see recent sync events and act on errors.
