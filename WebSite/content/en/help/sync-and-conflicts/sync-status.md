## Purpose

See whether your notes are synced, and when they last were.

## Prerequisites

You are signed in to iCloud.

## Steps

1. Look at the sync icon in the toolbar of any screen: green means synced, a spinning icon means syncing, an orange warning with a count means conflicts need you, and grey means offline.
2. Choose the icon to open **Sync Status**.
3. Read **Needs Your Attention** for conflicts, and **Recent Activity** for the log. The last-synced time stays visible when you are offline.

## Expected result

You know whether your notes are up to date.
