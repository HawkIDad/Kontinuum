## Purpose

Understand where templates are offered when you create a note.

## Prerequisites

At least one template group.

## Steps

1. Create a note (All Notes, **+**, or **+** inside a notebook).
2. In **Choose a Template**, see your template groups and their templates.
3. Pick one, or choose **Start Blank (no template)**.

{% admonition "note" %}This version of NoteBytez does not yet have a screen for attaching a template group to a specific notebook or to the journal.{% endadmonition %}

## Expected result

The note is created from the template you chose.
