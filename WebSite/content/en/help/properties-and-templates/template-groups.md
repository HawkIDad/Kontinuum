## Purpose

Create a group to hold related note templates.

## Prerequisites

A library is open.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Templates**.
2. Under **New Group**, type a name and choose **Add**.
3. Choose the group to open it.

## Expected result

The group appears in the list, ready for templates.
