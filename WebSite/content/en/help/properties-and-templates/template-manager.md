## Purpose

Review and delete your template groups and templates.

## Prerequisites

You have template groups.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Templates**.
2. Open a group to see its templates, then open a template to see its fields.
3. Swipe a group or template to delete it.

## Expected result

You can see and tidy everything you have set up.
