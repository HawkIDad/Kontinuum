## Purpose

Apply a newer version of a pack you have already added.

## Prerequisites

You added a pack, and a newer version is available.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**, then **Template Gallery**. Packs with a newer version show an **Update** badge.
2. Open the pack.
3. Choose **Update Available — Apply**.

## Expected result

The pack is updated in your library.
