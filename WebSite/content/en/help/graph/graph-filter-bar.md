## Purpose

Show only the notes you care about, and keep the filter.

## Prerequisites

The graph is open in Force mode.

## Steps

1. Choose **Filters** in the toolbar.
2. Set the depth, switch node types on or off (**Notes**, **Open Tasks**, **Orphans**) and optionally scope by tag.
3. Choose **Save as View**, name it and choose **Save**.

## Expected result

The graph shows only matching notes, and the filter is saved under Saved Views.
