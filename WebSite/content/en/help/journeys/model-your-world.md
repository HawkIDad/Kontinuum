## Model your world

1. **Templates.** [Create a template group](/en/help/properties-and-templates/template-groups/) and [build a note template](/en/help/properties-and-templates/note-templates/).
2. **Properties.** [Add a property to a note](/en/help/properties-and-templates/add-properties/).
3. **Canvas.** [Create a board](/en/help/canvas/create-canvas-board/) and [bind it to a note](/en/help/canvas/canvas-bind-note/).
4. **Saved views.** [Save a search or filter](/en/help/search-and-navigation/saved-views/).
5. **Insights.** [Review Graph Insights](/en/help/graph/graph-insights/) to see orphans, hubs and clusters.
