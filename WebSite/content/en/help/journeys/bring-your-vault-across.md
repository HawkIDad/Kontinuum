## Bring your vault across

1. **Import.** [Import an Obsidian vault](/en/help/migration/import-obsidian/) or [a Logseq graph](/en/help/migration/import-logseq/).
2. **Verify.** [Check your import](/en/help/migration/verify-import/) and see [how originals are kept](/en/help/migration/originals-archive/).
3. **Choose conflict handling.** [Choose a conflict strategy](/en/help/sync-and-conflicts/choose-conflict-strategy/) after reading the [comparison](/en/help/concepts/conflict-strategies-compared/).
4. **Stay portable.** Your notes are [plain Markdown](/en/help/concepts/plain-markdown-data/), and you can [export your library](/en/help/markdown-import-export/export-library/) at any time.
