## Your first week

1. **Set up.** [Install NoteBytez](/en/help/getting-started/install-and-platforms/) and [create a library](/en/help/getting-started/create-select-library/). Then [choose your kinds of work](/en/help/getting-started/onboarding-role-picker/).
2. **Capture.** [Write in today's journal](/en/help/capture-and-journal/todays-journal/) and use [quick capture](/en/help/capture-and-journal/quick-capture/) whenever an idea arrives.
3. **Link.** [Link two notes](/en/help/linking/wikilinks/) and check the [Backlinks pane](/en/help/linking/backlinks-pane/).
4. **Tag.** [Tag a note](/en/help/tags/add-tags/) and [browse your tags](/en/help/tags/tag-browser/).
5. **Find things.** [Search your library](/en/help/search-and-navigation/full-text-search/) and [jump with the Quick Switcher](/en/help/search-and-navigation/quick-switcher/).
6. **Trust sync.** [Check sync status](/en/help/sync-and-conflicts/sync-status/) and read [how syncing works](/en/help/concepts/sync-model/).
