## Share a space

1. **Share.** [Share a library](/en/help/sharing/share-library/) and [invite people](/en/help/sharing/invite-participants/).
2. **Set access.** [Choose read-only or read and write](/en/help/sharing/permission-levels/) for each person.
3. **Understand conflicts.** Read [the three strategies compared](/en/help/concepts/conflict-strategies-compared/).
4. **Resolve.** [Resolve a conflict](/en/help/sync-and-conflicts/resolve-conflicts/) and see [multi-party conflicts](/en/help/sharing/multi-party-conflicts/).
