## Purpose

Open the list of notes that share a tag.

## Prerequisites

At least one note has a tag.

## Steps

1. Open the tag browser, or tap a tag chip in a note to open it.
2. Choose a tag.
3. Choose a note in the list to open it.

## Expected result

You see, and can open, every note with that tag.
