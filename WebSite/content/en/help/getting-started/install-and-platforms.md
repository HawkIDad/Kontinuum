## Purpose

Install NoteBytez on iPhone, iPad or Mac and understand how the layout differs on each.

## Prerequisites

An Apple device running a supported version of iOS, iPadOS or macOS, and an Apple Account signed in to the App Store. Sign in to iCloud if you want your notes to sync.

## Steps

1. Open the App Store on your iPhone, iPad or Mac and search for NoteBytez.
2. Choose **Get** and, when asked, start the free trial. NoteBytez is a subscription, billed through the App Store.
3. Open NoteBytez. On first launch you will create your first library.

## Expected result

NoteBytez opens on the library screen. On iPhone you get a tab bar (Today, Notebooks, Search, Explore, Settings); on iPad and Mac you get a sidebar with the same places plus All Notes, Tags, Graph, Insights, Tasks, Saved Views and Canvas.
