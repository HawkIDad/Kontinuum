## Purpose

Learn where the Journal, Notebooks, Search and the rest live on your device.

## Prerequisites

A library is open.

## Steps

1. On iPhone, use the tab bar at the bottom: **Today**, **Notebooks**, **Search**, **Explore** and **Settings**. Explore lists Graph, Insights, Tasks, Saved Views, Canvas and Tags, plus a Command Palette row.
2. On iPad and Mac, use the sidebar: **Today** under Capture; **Notebooks**, **All Notes** and **Tags** under Library; **Search**, **Graph**, **Insights**, **Tasks**, **Saved Views** and **Canvas** under Explore; and **Settings**.
3. Look for the sync icon in the toolbar of every screen. Choose it to see sync status.

{% screenshot "notebooks/notebook-browser-iphone-light.png", "The Notebooks screen on iPhone, with the tab bar at the bottom and the sync icon at the top right.", "Notebooks on iPhone." %}

## Expected result

You can reach every part of the app from the tab bar or the sidebar.
