## Purpose

Pick your kinds of work so NoteBytez adds matching note templates.

## Prerequisites

You have just created your first library. The role picker is shown once.

## Steps

1. On the screen **What do you use NoteBytez for?**, tick the kinds of work that apply to you.
2. Choose **Add** (the button shows how many groups it will add), or choose **Skip** to add nothing.
3. To see or add packs later, open Settings and choose **Template Gallery**.

## Expected result

Matching template groups are added to your library and appear when you create a note.
