## Purpose

Handle a note that two people edited at once.

## Prerequisites

A shared library. Sync Status shows **This library is shared**.

## Steps

1. Open **Sync Status** when the sync icon shows a conflict.
2. Choose the conflict and resolve it as you would any other.
3. If you use **Keep All Versions**, choose **Keep Both Versions** when you are not sure whose edit to keep.

## Expected result

The conflict is resolved and both people's work is preserved as you chose.
