## Purpose

Invite someone by email.

## Prerequisites

The sharing sheet is open.

## Steps

1. Under **Add by Email**, type the person's email address.
2. Choose a **Permission**: **Read & Write** or **Read Only**.
3. Choose **Add**. You can also choose **Invite Participant** to use the system invitation sheet.
4. Choose **Done**.

## Expected result

The person is listed under **Participants** and can accept the invitation.
