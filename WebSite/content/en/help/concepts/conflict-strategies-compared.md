## Which strategy should I choose?

| Strategy | Who decides | What you do | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Keep All Versions** | You, every time | Compare the versions and keep one or both | You never want an edit decided for you |
| **Last-Write-Wins + Banner** | The newest edit | Nothing, unless you want to undo (the banner has **Revert**) | Mostly one person on several devices |
| **Markdown Diff-Merge** | NoteBytez merges the text | Review the diff and save the merged note | Notes that change in different places on different devices |

## Can I change my mind?

Yes. Choose a different strategy any time under **Settings**, **Sync & Conflicts**. It applies to future conflicts.

## Is anything ever lost silently?

No. NoteBytez never silently loses an edit: either you choose, or a banner lets you revert.
