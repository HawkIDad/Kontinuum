## How does NoteBytez know I am subscribed?

NoteBytez asks the App Store. A free trial, a paid subscription, Family Sharing and Apple's billing grace period all count as subscribed.

## What are the access levels?

- **Full** — everything works.
- **Warning** — your subscription has lapsed. You keep access for a 3-day grace period, with a banner and a **Resubscribe** button.
- **Blocked** — the grace period is over, you never subscribed, NoteBytez has not been able to verify your subscription for too long, or this copy cannot be verified as an App Store install.

## Can I work offline?

Yes. After a successful check NoteBytez trusts your subscription offline for up to 7 days.

## Are my notes ever deleted?

No. In every blocked state your notes are untouched and you can export all of them as Markdown. Resubscribing restores full access with no data loss.
