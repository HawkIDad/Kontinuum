## Purpose

Read or add to earlier days in your journal.

## Prerequisites

You are on the Today screen.

## Steps

1. Find the date at the top of the page, with a left and a right chevron.
2. Choose the left chevron to go to the previous day.
3. Choose the right chevron to move forward. It is disabled once you reach today.

## Expected result

The page shows the entry for the chosen day.
