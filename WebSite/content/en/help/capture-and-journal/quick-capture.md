## Purpose

Get an idea down now and file it properly later.

## Prerequisites

A library is open.

## Steps

1. Open **Today** and start typing straight away.
2. When an idea deserves its own note, choose **Promote to Notebook** in the toolbar and pick the block.
3. To jump to an existing note without leaving the journal, choose **Quick Switcher** in the toolbar.

## Expected result

The thought is safely in today's journal, and you can promote it later.
