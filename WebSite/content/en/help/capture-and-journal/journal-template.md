## Purpose

Understand the template that new journal pages start from.

## Prerequisites

A library is open.

## Steps

1. Open **Today** on a day that has no entry yet.
2. The new page starts from the basic journal template.
3. Write your entry as normal.

## Expected result

A new journal page for the day is ready, based on the template.
