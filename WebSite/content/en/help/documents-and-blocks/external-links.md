## Purpose

Link to a web page with standard Markdown.

## Prerequisites

A note is open in edit mode.

## Steps

1. Type `[link text](https://example.com)`, replacing the text and address.
2. Choose **Preview**.
3. Tap or click the link. It opens in your system browser.

## Expected result

The link shows as tappable text and opens the page in your browser.
