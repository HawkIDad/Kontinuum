## Purpose

Create a note, optionally from a template.

## Prerequisites

A library is open.

## Steps

1. On iPad and Mac, choose **All Notes** and then **+** (or press Command-N). On iPhone, open **Notebooks**, open a notebook and choose **+**.
2. In **Choose a Template**, pick a template, or choose **Start Blank (no template)**.
3. Type the note's title in the **Title** field, then write in the body.

{% admonition "note" %}A note created inside a notebook is filed in that notebook.{% endadmonition %}

## Expected result

The new note opens for editing, and appears in All Notes and in its notebook.
