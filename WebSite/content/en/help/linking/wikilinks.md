## Purpose

Link one note to another by typing its title in double brackets.

## Prerequisites

At least two notes exist in your library.

## Steps

1. In a note in edit mode, type `[[`. Suggestions appear above the bottom bar as you type.
2. Choose the note you want from the suggestions to insert the link.
3. Choose **Preview**, then tap the link to open the linked note.

## Expected result

The link is inserted, and the target note lists your note under Backlinks.
