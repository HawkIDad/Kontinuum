## Purpose

Refer to one block of another note with a block reference.

## Prerequisites

The note you want to refer to exists.

## Steps

1. In edit mode, type `((`. Blocks appear as suggestions, each with its heading path.
2. Choose a block to insert `((anchor))`.
3. Open the target note's **Backlinks** and look under **Block References** to see who refers to it.

## Expected result

The reference points at that block, and the target lists it under Block References.
