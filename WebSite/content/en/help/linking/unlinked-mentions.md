## Purpose

Find notes that mention this note's title without linking to it.

## Prerequisites

A note is open.

## Steps

1. Choose **Backlinks** at the bottom of the note.
2. Scroll to **Unlinked Mentions**. These are notes whose text contains this note's title without a link.
3. Tap a row to open that note and add the link.

## Expected result

You can turn plain mentions into real links.
