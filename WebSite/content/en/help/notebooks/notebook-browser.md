## Purpose

See all notebooks and open one.

## Prerequisites

A library is open.

## Steps

1. Open **Notebooks** (under Library on iPad and Mac).
2. Read the list; each row shows the notebook's name and its number of documents.
3. Choose a notebook to see its notes.

{% screenshot "notebooks/notebook-browser-iphone-light.png", "The Notebooks list on iPhone showing two notebooks with two documents each.", "Notebooks on iPhone." %}

## Expected result

You see your notebooks and can open any of them.
