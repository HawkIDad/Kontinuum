## Purpose

Put notes, images or web links on a board as cards.

## Prerequisites

A board is open.

## Steps

1. Choose **Add Card** in the toolbar.
2. Choose **Add Note…**, **Add Media…** or **Add Web Link…**.
3. Pick the note or media, or type the web address, and confirm.
4. Drag a card to move it, and resize it as needed.

## Expected result

The card appears on the board.
