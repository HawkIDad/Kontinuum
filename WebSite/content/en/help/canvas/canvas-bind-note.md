## Purpose

Tie a board to a note so the two stay related.

## Prerequisites

A board and a note exist.

## Steps

1. Open the board and choose **Bind to Note…** in the toolbar (or press and hold the board in the list).
2. Search for and choose the note.
3. To remove the binding, choose **Unbind**.

## Expected result

The board shows a badge with the note's title, and the note can be opened as a bound canvas.
