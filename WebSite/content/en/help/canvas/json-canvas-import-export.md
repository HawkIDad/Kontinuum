## Purpose

Move boards in and out of NoteBytez as `.canvas` files.

## Prerequisites

A library is open. To import, you have a `.canvas` file.

## Steps

1. To import, open **Canvas** and choose **Import Canvas…**, then pick the file.
2. To export, open a board and choose **Export Canvas…**, then choose where to save it.

## Expected result

The board is imported into your library, or saved as a `.canvas` file.
