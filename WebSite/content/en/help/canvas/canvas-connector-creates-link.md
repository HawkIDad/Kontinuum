## Purpose

Drawing a connector between two note cards on a bound board can create a wikilink.

## Prerequisites

A board bound to a note, with two note cards.

## Steps

1. Choose the connect toggle and connect the two note cards.
2. When **Create Link?** appears, choose **Create Link** to write the `[[link]]`, or **Cancel**.

## Expected result

With Create Link, the link is written into the note. With Cancel, no connector is created.
