## Purpose

Draw labelled connectors between cards and gather cards into groups.

## Prerequisites

A board with at least two cards.

## Steps

1. Choose the connect toggle in the toolbar, then choose two cards to connect them.
2. To add a group, choose **Add Card**, then **Add Group…**, name it and choose **Add**.

## Expected result

Cards are joined by a line, and groups outline related cards.
