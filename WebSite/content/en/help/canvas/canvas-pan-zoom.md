## Purpose

Move around a large board.

## Prerequisites

A board is open.

## Steps

1. Drag the background to pan.
2. Use the zoom in and zoom out buttons to change scale.
3. Choose fit to screen to see every card.

## Expected result

The whole board is visible at a comfortable scale.
