## Purpose

Limit a search to note text, tags or note titles.

## Prerequisites

The Search screen is open.

## Steps

1. Choose a scope chip: **Content** (note text), **Tag** or **Path** (note titles).
2. Type your query.
3. Choose a result to open it.

## Expected result

Results match only in the scope you chose.
