## Purpose

Open any note by typing part of its title.

## Prerequisites

A library with some notes.

## Steps

1. On Today, choose **Quick Switcher** in the toolbar. On iPad and Mac you can also press Command-O.
2. Type part of the title.
3. Choose the note to open it.

## Expected result

The note opens.
