## Purpose

Find notes by what they contain.

## Prerequisites

A library with some notes.

## Steps

1. Open **Search**. On iPad and Mac it is under Explore.
2. Type in the search box.
3. Choose a result to open the note.

## Expected result

Results list the matching notes with a snippet, ranked by relevance.
