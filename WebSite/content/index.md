## Who it is for

- **Coming from Apple Notes or files.** Start with the journal, then add links and tags as you go.
- **Switching from Obsidian or Logseq.** The Migration Assistant brings your vault across and keeps the originals as a read-only archive.
- **Building a system of notes.** Properties, templates, saved views, the graph and the canvas help you model a project or a subject.
- **Sharing a space.** Invite people to a library and choose who can read and who can edit.

## Your notes stay yours

NoteBytez keeps your notes on your devices and syncs them through your own iCloud account. There is no NoteBytez server that holds a copy. Notes are plain Markdown, and you can export a note or your whole library at any time.
