## About this website

This website is a static site. It uses **no analytics and no cookies**, and it has no forms or accounts. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs of requests, as any web host does. Search on this site runs in your browser and sends nothing to us.

## How NoteBytez handles your data

NoteBytez keeps your notes on your devices and in your own iCloud account through Apple CloudKit, and works local-first. KwickSync operates no server that receives your notes. The full Data Use Policy follows; it is the same text shown in the app.
