## Purpose
Create a wikilink between two notes.

## Prerequisites
Two notes exist in your library.

## Steps
1. Type `[[` in a note and choose a target from the list.
2. Press Return to insert the link.

{% admonition "tip" %}Autocomplete matches on title as you type.{% endadmonition %}

## How do I follow a link?
Tap or click it; the target note opens. See {% helpLink "linking", "sample-howto-two", "the second sample" %}.

## Expected result
The link appears styled and the target lists this note under Backlinks.
